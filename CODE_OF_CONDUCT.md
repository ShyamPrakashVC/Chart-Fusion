# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful and professional collaboration environment for everyone who contributes to ChartFusion.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.
- Keep discussions relevant to the visual, documentation, quality, and user needs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional workplace.

## Reporting

Report concerns through the project support contact listed in [docs/SUPPORT.md](docs/SUPPORT.md).

Maintainers should review reports promptly and take action that is fair, proportionate, and consistent with organization policy.
